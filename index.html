OUR WORK

Activities

Business Process Management activities completed by Team 3.

Activity 1

This activity shows Business Process Management (BPM) and how it helps Foodpanda organize and improve its business processes. It focuses on making the processes more efficient, consistent, and customer-focused through continuous improvement.

Activity 2

This activity shows the Foodpanda Business Process Architecture Diagram. It organizes Foodpanda's operations into management, core, and support processes.

Activity 2 Revised Work

This activity shows the revised Foodpanda Business Process Architecture Diagram. It presents the management, core, and support processes and clearly shows how they are connected.

Activity 2 Part 2

This activity shows how Foodpanda can use BPM strategies to address operational problems such as delayed deliveries, wrong orders, and customer support issues.

Activity 3

This activity shows how Foodpanda identifies and analyzes its business processes, focusing on the Delivery Process as the priority process. It also defines the process scope and key stakeholders.

Activity 4

This activity shows the Foodpanda Delivery Process using a BPMN diagram. It illustrates how the restaurant, IT team, delivery rider, customer support team, and customer work together.

Activity 4 Revised Work

This activity shows the revised Foodpanda Delivery Process BPMN diagram. It illustrates the flow between the customer, restaurant partners, IT team, rider app, and delivery rider.

Activity 5

This activity shows the Foodpanda Fishbone Diagram for analyzing the causes of late deliveries or delayed orders. It identifies six main categories: People, Process, Technology, Product, Prices & Promotion, and External Factors.

Activity 6

This activity shows the Process Improvement Proposal for Casita Del Sol Resort and Events Place. It analyzes the current event booking process, identifies its problems and possible root causes using an interview and Fishbone Diagram, and proposes improvements to reduce waiting, repeated work, errors, and unnecessary process activities.

Activity 7

This activity shows the Balanced Scorecard and KPI Table for Casita Del Sol Private Resort and Events Place. It evaluates customer service performance across four perspectives: Financial, Customer, Internal Business Process, and Learning & Growth. It identifies the objectives, Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), targets, monitoring frequency, and responsible personnel to measure performance and improve the quality and efficiency of customer service.
